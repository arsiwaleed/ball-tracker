Stream & Output Settings

You can use the virtually panned camera output in several high-performance ways:

1. Recording High-Quality Clamps

Tap the red circle button at the bottom center to record the canvas stream live to your phone. We record the fully tracked frame in standard WebM/MP4 format which download instantly when you stop!

2. Cast to OBS Studio / Live Stream

To use this as a live panning camera source for OBS or YouTube/Twitch streams:

  1. Open this app on a phone connected to the same local Wi-Fi.
  2. Toggle off the bounding boxes to keep the stream clean.
  3. Share the phone screen using AirPlay/Chromecast, or load this page inside OBS using a WebRTC broadcast link.
  4. Recommended: Connect this canvas output directly to VDO.ninja or OBS.Ninja as an instant browser capture source.

3. PWA (Mobile Native Mode)

Tap your browser's share icon (iOS Safari) or menu icon (Android Chrome) and select "Add to Home Screen". This launches the application in an immersive, full-screen standalone mode without URL bars, mimicking a native mobile camera app.
